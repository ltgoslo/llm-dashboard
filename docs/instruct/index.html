About NorEval Instruct

NorEval is a comprehensive evaluation benchmark for Norwegian language models, described in Mikhailov et al. (2025). This page shows a pilot of its generative version for instruction-tuned models (NorEval 1.3): 32 tasks spanning linguistic knowledge, language understanding, world knowledge & reasoning, generation & summarization, and translation; each asked as a chat message. The models then answer by freely generating full chat responses.

Each task is evaluated on 1,000 draws: an item of the task's dataset with one of its prompts, both picked at random, and the same draws for every model. A model answers each draw once, sampling at temperature 1 (up to 4,096 tokens; 16,384 for a model that reasons first). NorEval's minimal-pair tasks (NCB, NoCoLA, MultiBLiMP) are left out, as they have no prompt to answer.

The judge

An LLM judge, DeepSeek V4 Flash, grades every response from 0 (incorrect) to 5 (fully correct) against the dataset's reference, with one rubric for all tasks. Before grading, it reads the prompt and the reference, and explains its grade first.

Confidence intervals

                                
Error bars are 95% intervals: the score ± 1.96 standard errors, with a task's items counted as a sample of all the items the task could have
                                    (the draws' errors clustered by item). A task with few items stays uncertain however often they are drawn: NorSumm's 33 texts, about 30 draws each, leave about ±0.2.
                                    The intervals cover which items and prompts were drawn and the model's and the judge's sampling, not the choice of judge, rubric or prompts.
                                    Averages over tasks combine the tasks' intervals in quadrature.
